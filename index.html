Volume
Hitsounds

A skin with its own hitsounds is used instead of the default sounds (Settings → Skin).

Playback
Playback speed

Slower speeds lower the pitch of the song.

Sync

Calibration measures your headphones' delay, then lets you line up the notes on screen and the hitsounds with the song.

·

Visual offset moves only what you see: positive = notes appear later (if they look ahead of the music). It doesn't change any sound.

Playfield
On screen

Auto cursor, score and judgements show only while previewing (playing the map), not in Modding.

Background
Screen
Performance

Lower frame rates save battery and heat on slower phones. Your screen's refresh rate is the upper limit.

Storyboard

WebGL draws a big storyboard on the graphics chip, in a few steps instead of one per sprite. Over a map's video, or where the browser can't do it, the canvas is used. With Quality on Auto, a heavy storyboard also leaves out its faintest, tiniest sprites while the screen can't keep up.

Watching a replay

These switches are also under the eye button on the bottom bar while a replay plays. Top 50 needs Score / combo.

Colours

From the map: the colours the mapper chose (the skin's when the map has none). From the skin: always the skin's combo colours.
