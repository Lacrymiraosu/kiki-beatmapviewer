A. CODE TRANSLATED FROM OR FOLLOWING OTHER PROJECTS

1. ppy/osu (osu!lazer) - https://github.com/ppy/osu
   Licence: MIT.  Stored here: no. The logic was rewritten in JavaScript and
   behaves the same (a translation, so this notice has to stay with it):
   - js/parse.js: object stacking (OsuBeatmapProcessor), slider paths
     (SliderPath, Legacy/ConvertHitObjectParser)
   - js/auto.js: Auto's replay (OsuAutoGenerator)
   - js/replay.js: the .osr reading rules (LegacyScoreDecoder: frame parsing,
     the version that tells a lazer replay from a stable one)
   - js/replayana.js: the replay analysis follows lazer's judgement and tracking rules
     (SliderInputManager, HitResult and the combo rules it cites)
   - js/verify.js: ScoreV1 as lazer's OsuLegacyScoreSimulator computes it
   - js/skins.js: the slider body's border drawing (osu-framework SmoothPath)
   Short pointers to this file are in the header of each of those files.

F. CONTENT THAT USERS UPLOAD

9. Online projects and share links hold what their users uploaded; it belongs
   to its mappers, artists and owners, not to this project. The site only
   keeps it for the owner and for the people the owner shares it with. To have
   something taken down, use "Report a problem" at the bottom of any page.

   Files a user opens or imports on their own device (.osz and .osu maps,
   .osr replays, and .osk skins, which are kept only in that browser) stay in
   their browser and belong to their authors; this project does not receive
   them unless the user chooses "Save online" or a live session.
